# Bluetooth Item Trackers: Specs, Platforms and Prices (as of 2026-10-19)

Scope: Apple AirTag (1st and 2nd gen), Samsung Galaxy SmartTag2 (and successor rumors), Tile (Mate, Pro, Slim, Sticker), Chipolo (ONE Spot, POP, ONE Point, CARD Point), Pebblebee (Clip, Card, Tag, Universal), Motorola Moto Tag / Moto Tag 2, Eufy SmartTrack Link, and Android Find Hub trackers.

Confidence key: **[C]** confirmed by at least one primary or reputable source; **[U]** uncertain (sources conflict, or only a reseller/vendor page). Prices are as seen in sources dated between 2023 and 2026; none were checked on official store pages in this session. Re-check before publishing.

Source numbers (S1 to S32) are listed at the end of the file. Each finding also carries an inline link.

### Cited Findings

#### Apple AirTag 2
- Apple AirTag 2 launched in early January 2026 at the same price as the original, $29 for one or $99 for four, with a 2nd-gen UWB chip, an upgraded Bluetooth chip and a speaker about 50% louder; Precision Finding and Bluetooth range are up to 1.5x longer. [Mactrast](https://www.mactrast.com/2026/01/apple-unveils-second-generation-airtag-with-longer-bluetooth-range-louder-speaker-more/); [Shi listing summary, S1]
- Same CR2032 battery rated about one year, IP67; battery life did not improve; no mounting hardware included. [Expert Reviews](https://www.expertreviews.co.uk/technology/accessories/apple-airtag-2nd-gen-review)
- Requires iOS 26.2.1 / iPadOS 26.2.1 / watchOS 26.2.1 or later. [Mactrast](https://www.mactrast.com/2026/01/apple-unveils-second-generation-airtag-with-longer-bluetooth-range-louder-speaker-more/)
- Launch date conflicts across sources (23 Jan vs 26 Jan 2026). [Mactrast](https://www.mactrast.com/2026/01/apple-unveils-second-generation-airtag-with-longer-bluetooth-range-louder-speaker-more/)
- UK price £29 / £99 four-pack; Singapore S$45 / S$149; India Rs.3,790 / Rs.12,900. [Expert Reviews](https://www.expertreviews.co.uk/technology/accessories/apple-airtag-2nd-gen-review)

#### Galaxy SmartTag2 (Samsung)
- Samsung regional pages describe IP67 (1 m freshwater, 30 min), replaceable CR2032, "up to 500 days" battery claim from lab simulation. [Samsung AU](https://www.samsung.com/au/mobile-accessories/galaxy-smarttag2-white-ei-t5600bwegau); [Samsung PH](https://www.samsung.com/ph/mobile-accessories/galaxy-smarttag2-black-ei-t5600bbegww/)
- Retailer listing: BLE 5.3, range up to 120 m over BLE, one button, speaker buzzer, requires Android 9+; UWB features need UWB-capable Galaxy devices. [Harvey Norman](https://www.harveynorman.co.uk/products/samsung-galaxy-smart-tag-2-black)
- Brazilian article at launch claims battery "up to 700 days", which conflicts with Samsung's 500-day figure. [Hardware.com.br](https://www.hardware.com.br/mobile/samsung-lanca-o-galaxy-smarttag-2-com-bateria-que-dura-ate-700-dias/amp/)
- Launch price US$29.99. [Hardware.com.br](https://www.hardware.com.br/mobile/samsung-lanca-o-galaxy-smarttag-2-com-bateria-que-dura-ate-700-dias/amp/)
- Works only with Samsung Galaxy devices; no UWB in SmartTag2. [Telefonino/Notebookcheck summary, S10]

#### Samsung successor (SmartTag 3) - rumor only

[Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Samsung-s-SmartTag-3-rumored-to-be-launching-alongside-S26-FE.1339851.0.html); [9to5Google, 14 Jul 2026](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/14/samsung-galaxy-smarttag-3-reportedly-coming-later-this-year/)

#### Chipolo
- Chipolo support: ONE Point, CARD Point, POP, LOOP and CARD are Find Hub compatible. ONE Spot and CARD Spot are Apple Find My only. [Chipolo support](https://support.chipolo.net/hc/en-us/articles/18461727113501-Which-Chipolo-products-work-with-Google-s-Find-Hub-app)
- ONE Spot specs from a reseller listing: about 60 m sound range, 37.9 mm x 6.4 mm, IPX5, replaceable CR2032 up to about 1 yr, up to 120 dB (reseller only). [Reseller, S16]; Size confirmed by [Chipolo support](https://support.chipolo.net/hc/en-us/articles/212176025-How-big-is-Chipolo)
- POP: works with Apple Find My or Google's Find My Device, but only one network at a time. [Park Cameras](https://www.parkcameras.com/shop/chipolo-pop-tracking-tag-green_9709810c)
- POP: IP55, maximum 120 dB, list €34.99, sales €23.99-28.90 (Germany). [Coolblue](https://www.coolblue.de/en/product/967320/chipolo-pop-green.html)
- POP described by Tom's Guide as one of the loudest trackers, 10 dB louder than Tile Pro; it is IP55 (AirTags are IP67). [Tom's Guide](https://tomsguide.com/home/smart-home/forget-airtags-chipolos-new-item-trackers-are-louder-and-work-with-both-apple-and-google)

- Bluetooth version conflicts: Coolblue says 5.1, Park Cameras says BLE 6.0. [Coolblue](https://www.coolblue.de/en/product/967320/chipolo-pop-green.html); [Park Cameras](https://www.parkcameras.com/shop/chipolo-pop-tracking-tag-green_9709810c)

[Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/acessorios/airtag-da-motorola-novo-rastreador-moto-tag-2-chega-ao-brasil-veja-preco/)
- Original Moto Tag in Brazil: R$299, R$269.10 with Pix or card cash. [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/acessorios/airtag-da-motorola-novo-rastreador-moto-tag-2-chega-ao-brasil-veja-preco/)

[Soundcore/Eufy spec page](https://service.soundcore.com/article-description/Introducing-eufy-SmartTrack-Link)
- Launch price $19.99; 2026 vendor comparison gives $14-18 but is promotional. [AirPinpoint](https://airpinpoint.com/compare/airtags-vs-eufy)

## Key Question 2: Platform support (iPhone only, Android only, or both)

### Takeaway
Apple-only: AirTag 1 and 2, Chipolo ONE Spot. Android-only: Moto Tag and Moto Tag 2, Chipolo ONE Point and CARD Point, Samsung SmartTag2 (Galaxy only). Both: Pebblebee Universal line, Chipolo POP (one network at a time), Eufy SmartTrack Link (Android with limited alerts). Tile platform status is unverified.

### Cited Findings
- Apple Find My: AirTag 1 and 2, Chipolo ONE Spot. [Chipolo support](https://support.chipolo.net/hc/en-us/articles/18461727113501-Which-Chipolo-products-work-with-Google-s-Find-Hub-app)
- Google Find Hub (Android 9+ for Chipolo): Moto Tag / Moto Tag 2, Chipolo ONE Point, CARD Point; Chipolo POP switchable. [Chipolo support](https://support.chipolo.net/hc/en-us/articles/18461727113501-Which-Chipolo-products-work-with-Google-s-Find-Hub-app); [Motorola BR/Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/acessorios/airtag-da-motorola-novo-rastreador-moto-tag-2-chega-ao-brasil-veja-preco/)
- Samsung SmartTag2 works only on Samsung Galaxy devices. [Telefonino summary, S10](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Samsung-s-SmartTag-3-rumored-to-be-launching-alongside-S26-FE.1339851.0.html)
- Pebblebee Universal: both Apple Find My and Google Find Hub. [BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20241001557140/en/Introducing-Pebblebee-Universal-The-Worlds-First-Item-Trackers-Compatible-with-Both-Apple-Find-My-and-Google-Find-My-Device-Networks)
- Eufy SmartTrack Link: Apple Find My and Android via eufy app. [Soundcore/Eufy](https://service.soundcore.com/article-description/Introducing-eufy-SmartTrack-Link)

### Inferences
- For iPhone-plus-Android households, Pebblebee Universal and Chipolo POP are the only two options with dual-network support in this research. Eufy also works on both, but with reduced Android alerts.

### Gaps
- Tile: not verified whether current models work with Find My or Find Hub, or only in the Tile app.
- Xiaomi and other Find Hub trackers were not researched.
